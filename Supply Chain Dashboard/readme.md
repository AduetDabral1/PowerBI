## 📌 Overview

This Power BI dashboard delivers a comprehensive, end-to-end view of supply chain operations, enabling data-driven decision-making across procurement, inventory, logistics, and customer fulfillment.

It is designed to answer critical operational and strategic questions such as:

Where are bottlenecks occurring in the supply chain?
Which suppliers are underperforming?
Are inventory levels optimized or leading to stockouts/overstocking?
How efficient are shipments and deliveries?
How does customer demand align with supply performance?
